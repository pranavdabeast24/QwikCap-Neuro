# Use research data safely

QwikCap is a research prototype.
Use fictional records until your institution approves the software and workflow for real patient data.
Local processing alone does not establish compliance or clinical suitability.

The app does not provide managed user access, encryption at rest, or a tamper-evident audit trail.
Review each suggested answer against its source.
Live REDCap import has not been validated.

Saved projects contain source documents and answers.
Exports remain where you save them.
A crash can leave temporary files.
Use approved storage and retention rules.

Never put patient records, credentials, private datasets, or screenshots of real records in GitHub.
The included synthetic packets and named example exports are approved exceptions.

Report a suspected vulnerability through an established private channel with the repository owner.
Use a small fictional example.
Include the affected version and steps to reproduce the issue.
Do not send real records or publish exploit details.
